Qmoosa Pi Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026

Qmoosa Pi is a Pi Network application designed to provide Pi-native authentication, payment interactions, and related application features. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use Qmoosa Pi.

1. Information we process

When you authenticate through the official Pi SDK, Qmoosa Pi may receive information authorized by you through Pi Network, such as your Pi user identifier, username, and wallet address where available and permitted. Qmoosa Pi may also process payment identifiers and blockchain transaction identifiers needed to approve, complete, reconcile, or troubleshoot Pi payments.

2. How information is used

Information is used to authenticate users, provide requested application features, process and verify Pi transactions, maintain security, prevent abuse, and diagnose technical problems.

3. Authentication and payments

Qmoosa Pi uses the official Pi SDK for Pi authentication and payment initiation. Server-side requests may be made to Pi Platform APIs to verify identity and process payment approval or completion. Qmoosa Pi does not ask users to provide wallet seed phrases or private keys.

4. Data sharing

Qmoosa Pi does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by infrastructure providers that host the application and backend services, and by Pi Network where required to provide Pi authentication and payment functionality.

5. Data retention

Qmoosa Pi aims to retain only information reasonably necessary to provide and secure the service. Some technical or transaction records may be retained where required for security, fraud prevention, accounting, dispute handling, or legal compliance.

6. Security

Reasonable technical safeguards are used to protect application data. However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security. Never share your Pi wallet passphrase, seed phrase, or private keys with Qmoosa Pi or any third party.

7. Children

Qmoosa Pi is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

8. Your choices

You may stop using Qmoosa Pi at any time. Permissions granted through Pi Network are subject to Pi Network's own account and platform controls.

9. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the application evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer using the contact information published in the Qmoosa Pi application listing or repository.
